About MovieScope

Academic & portfolio data science project showcasing full-stack Python analytics.

Technical Architecture & Implemented Principles

MovieScope is a production-style, standalone data science and recommendation engine built to demonstrate core Python, NumPy, Pandas, and Flask capabilities:

Python 3 & OOP

Modular service architecture split into DataManager, AnalyticsEngine, and MovieRecommender with strict separation of concerns.

Pandas Wrangling

Group-by aggregation, multi-genre exploding, outer and left merges, missing-value imputation, and deduplication.

NumPy Vectorization

Multi-hot matrix representations, vectorized cosine similarity calculations, and Bayesian weighted rating ranking without slow loops.

Matplotlib & Seaborn

Server-side analytical figure rendering with non-interactive Agg backend, thread safety, and direct image streaming.

Recommendation Algorithm Methodology

Weighted Rating (WR) = (v / (v + m)) × R + (m / (v + m)) × C

Where v is movie rating count, m is minimum rating threshold (5), R is movie average rating, and C is dataset mean rating.

Combined Score = 0.65 × Cosine Similarity + 0.35 × Normalized Weighted Rating
